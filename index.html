Quick eligibility check

BSN ASB Term Financing is open to all qualified ASB investors under ASNB guidelines, aged 18–70 at the end of the loan tenure.

ASB is a unit trust exclusively for Malaysian Bumiputera individuals as defined under the Federal Constitution, and open to Malaysian citizens holding a valid MyKad. An ASB account can be opened at any age (a minor's account is held in trust by a parent or guardian) — but this BSN financing product itself requires the applicant to be aged 18–70 at the end of the loan tenure.

Question 5 of 7

Would you like Takaful (Insurance) coverage?

Takaful protects your family by settling the outstanding financing in case of death or total permanent disability. It also comes with a slightly lower profit rate.

With Takaful

Financing protected against death / TPD.

SBR + 1.40% = 4.15% p.a.

Without Takaful

No insurance coverage on the financing.
